# Voice Contract

## Voice Interaction
- Start review session: "Let's begin your Daily Review."
- Next step prompt: "What would you like to capture today?"
- Clarification: "Can you tell me more about that?"
- Summary: "Here's a summary of your review."
- Pause/Resume: "Say 'pause' to stop, 'resume' to continue."

## Error Handling
- "I didn't catch that. Can you repeat?"
- "You can pause anytime."
